01 Regulatory Demarcation: SFC Circulars 24EC55 & 26EC32 for Licensed Corporations

The Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has established a robust supervisory perimeter for Generative AI adoption through Circulars 24EC55 ("Use of Generative AI by Licensed Corporations") and 26EC32 ("Cyber Resilience and Adversarial Guardrails in Algorithmic and Agentic Workflows").

These circulars establish that Licensed Corporations (LCs)—specifically those holding Type 1 (Dealing in Securities), Type 4 (Advising on Securities), and Type 9 (Asset Management) licenses—retain absolute fiduciary and regulatory responsibility for the accuracy, fairness, and security of all AI-generated or AI-assisted research and advice.

Under the SFC regulatory framework, technological complexity cannot be invoked to excuse compliance failures. LCs deploying large language models, agentic workflows, or automated research copilots must institute formal controls matching the speed and risk profile of automated synthesis.

02 The Manager-in-Charge (MIC) Regime: Non-Delegable Algorithmic Accountability

Under the SFC Manager-in-Charge (MIC) regime, algorithmic automation does not diminish executive culpability. Circular 24EC55 makes clear that designated MICs hold direct personal accountability:

MIC for Overall Oversight

Direct accountability for firm-wide AI strategy, risk governance, and regulatory escalation.

MIC for Risk Management

Independent validation of models, drift limits, hallucination tolerances, and model inventory.

MIC for Information Tech

Zero-cloud-egress guarantees, In-VPC hosting, container isolation, and MCP firewalling.

MIC for Compliance

Fiduciary adherence, Code of Conduct alignment, and client disclosure verification.

03 MNPI Containment & Data Loss Prevention (DLP) Boundaries

A central concern of SFC Circular 24EC55 is the accidental leakage or cross-contamination of Material Non-Public Information (MNPI). When research analysts or portfolio managers query an LLM regarding sensitive M&A transactions, earnings embargoes, or block trading interests, any data egress creates catastrophic regulatory exposure under Part XIII / XIV of the SFO (Cap. 571) regarding insider dealing.

  • Complete Data Egress Isolation: LLM inference engines must operate within dedicated private VPC boundaries where egress traffic to public endpoints is blocked at the routing layer.
  • Zero Retention Architecture: Cryptographic and contractual proof of zero model training, zero prompt logging, and zero retention on external infrastructure.
  • Chinese Wall Isolation: Multi-tenant vector databases must maintain cryptographic tenant separation ensuring research models cannot index private investment banking records.

04 Deterministic Citation Pinning & Hallucination Elimination Protocol

Under Circular 24EC55, investment advice or research distributed to institutional or retail clients cannot rely on unverified generative text. To satisfy the SFC's standard of reasonable basis and due diligence:

Deterministic Citation Pinning Workflow
1. Primary Source Ingestion

HKEX filings and audited financials tagged with cryptographic SHA-256 hashes.

2. Grounded Synthesis

Copilot synthesises commentary with mandatory markdown back-references to source chunks.

3. Licensed RO Sign-Off

Responsible Officer validates pinned evidence before client-facing dissemination.

05 Adversarial Robustness & Cyber Containment (Circular 26EC32)

Circular 26EC32 addresses the emerging threat landscape of agentic AI systems, focusing on cyber resilience against automated adversaries:

  • Indirect Prompt Injection: Malicious third parties embedding hidden prompt overrides in public financial disclosures, analyst PDF memos, or market data feeds. LCs must implement dual-model token verification and context-boundary sandboxing.
  • Model Context Protocol (MCP) Firewalls: When agentic copilots are granted tool-calling capabilities, all tools must be strictly read-only and governed by an immutable MCP schema registry. Arbitrary shell execution or outbound HTTP requests must be physically blocked.
  • Adversarial Red-Teaming: LCs must conduct recurring adversarial penetration testing before deploying agentic capabilities into production, verifying that jailbreak attempts cannot bypass MNPI filters or compliance boundaries.
