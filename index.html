Q3 Which Hong Kong supervisory authorities and guidelines are admitted into the baseline?

The active baseline maintains continuous statutory coverage across all four admitted Hong Kong financial regulatory authorities:

  • HKMA: Circular B1/15C (15 Oct 2026) "Supervisory Guidance for Responsible Adoption of Artificial Intelligence" (establishing the ART Framework across Principles 1–9), High-Level Principles on AI, and SPM TM-E-1.
  • SFC: Code of Conduct Schedule 7 (Algorithmic Trading & DMA) and Circular 24EC55 / 26EC32 on Generative AI.
  • PCPD: Artificial Intelligence: Model Personal Data Protection Framework under the PDPO (Cap. 486).
  • IA: Guideline on Enterprise Cybersecurity (GL20) and Guidelines GL14 / GL22.
